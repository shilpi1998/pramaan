HDFC's AI Council is evaluating Agentforce. Their Model Risk Management team asked detailed architecture questions. Then an RBI audit flagged that CI/CD code was sitting outside HDFC's network. The bank now wants a Source Code Review & an OSA report — but only from a CERT-In empaneled vendor. Until that's done, nothing moves.

IDFC First Bank
RBI Audit Crisis
RBI auditors showed up and asked for integrity statement evidence. IDFC couldn't produce it fast enough. 10,000 collections users were stopped while the bank scrambled. Push messaging was flagged. The compliance team needed answers within 2–3 hours — and didn't have them ready. "Non-compliance with RBI regulations could lead to penalties."
Impact: 10,000 users blocked, urgent 2-hour deadline
